two years later

eight things i've learned in two years

1. everyone knows a different version of me.

people meet you at different stages of life, under different circumstances, and often hold on to their prejudice impression. people will judge you, some will get you wrong, and you'll get others wrong too. and that's okay.

2. your world is not the world.

where you grow up shapes what you consider normal. living elsewhere makes you question assumptions you never realized you had.

3. independence and belonging aren't opposites.

i think humans are meant to be independent, and living on your own terms feels wonderful. but being away from home has made me appreciate how much meaning comes from staying connected to family. you can value freedom without wanting to be alone.

4. technology keeps getting more interesting.

technology has always had the power to change the world. but with all these frontier technologies, the scale of change feels even greater. ai has made experimenting with ideas remarkably easy. the more i explore, the more curious i become about what's possible.

5. try to be as articulate as possible.

people can misunderstand you even when you think you've been clear. what you mean and what others understand are often two different things.

6. don't reveal too much about yourself.

not everyone needs to know everything about you. being honest doesn't require saying everything, and being understood doesn't require being completely known. i've learned to be more careful about what i share and with whom.

7. be decisive.

internal conflicts can be more exhausting than the consequences of a decision. i've spent too much time weighing possibilities when i could have been moving forward. sometimes clarity comes from deciding, not from thinking longer.

i've also noticed that when i care about something, i care deeply. when i don't, i barely care at all, even when it's important. this tendency has sometimes worked against me.

8. two years is both a long time and no time at all.

i think time flies best in longer scale than short.
